A full year of Helsinki temperature predictions, built with NVIDIA Kumo in Snowflake.

The workflow is approachable: connect related tables → define a prediction in PQL → train on a GPU → save predictions back to Snowflake.

I tested it with Finnish Meteorological Institute data from three stations:
• Full 2025 history, plus earlier years for training
• 903 daily comparisons against 2026 actuals through October 28
• A separate recursive forecast, with November and December showing predictions only

The result is a working integration—and a useful reminder to keep the baseline visible. Kumo's average absolute error was 3.09°C; simply predicting yesterday's temperature achieved 1.45°C.

This was a three-epoch archival experiment, not a validated weather service. The notebook, data snapshot, package fixes, and results are all available in SnowKumo.
